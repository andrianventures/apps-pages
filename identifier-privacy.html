Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dmitriy Andrianov ("we," "us," "our"), based in the United States, handles information when you use Sunglasses Identifier or a similar identifier mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy together with our Terms of Use. Capitalized words used here have the same meaning as in the Terms unless we define them differently below.

We design the App to work with minimal personal data. We do not sell your personal information. What we process depends on which features you choose to use.

Information we process

Local data on your device. Sunglasses Identifier stores items such as your last 20 identifications (each containing a thumbnail of the photo you provided plus the AI-generated details), onboarding progress, daily-scan counters, and rating-prompt state using your device's storage. That information stays on the device until you delete the App, clear data, or tap Clear in History.

Photos you submit for identification. When you take a photo with the in-app camera or pick one from your photo library, the App sends the image to our backend (a Supabase Edge Function that we operate) which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI service for identification. The image is processed to generate a result and is not stored by us beyond what is necessary to return that result to your device. Google's processing of the image is governed by Google's terms and privacy notice (linked below). Only submit photos you are allowed to share.

Photos, face-related imagery, and biometrics

The App is for identifying sunglasses from photos. We do not use Apple Face ID, TrueDepth, ARKit face tracking, or any API whose purpose is to capture face geometry, face maps, or other biometric face templates. We do not create or store a separate “face print” or facial recognition profile.

If you photograph sunglasses while they are on a person—or your image otherwise includes a face—the picture you submit may contain incidental imagery of a face as part of the ordinary 2D photo. That image is handled only as described under Photos you submit for identification above: our Edge Function relays it to Google Gemini to produce sunglasses identification results; we do not keep a copy on our servers after the response is returned. Gemini’s retention and processing are covered by Google’s policies. Locally, a thumbnail may be saved in your History until you delete the App or clear History, as described under Local data on your device above.

Purchases. If you buy a subscription, the payment platform (for example, Apple) processes payment. We use service providers such as RevenueCat to validate entitlements and manage premium access. Those providers receive transaction-related identifiers and similar data needed to operate purchases.

Support email. If you email us, we receive your address, message content, and metadata your mail system sends (for example, time stamps).
