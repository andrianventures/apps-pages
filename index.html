Currency conversion apps

Pesos to Dollars Conversion + and Euros to Dollars Conversion + are fast USD ↔ MXN and USD ↔ EUR converters with live exchange rates, optional AI price-tag scanning from a photo, conversion history, and offline-friendly cached rates.

  • Legal: Privacy Policy · Terms of Use
  • Free tier: a limited number of conversions; premium unlocks unlimited conversions, AI scans, and ad-free use. See the in-app paywall for current plans and prices.
  • Subscriptions: managed through Apple (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions). Deleting an app does not cancel a subscription.

Our Identifier apps

Sunglasses Identifier uses AI to identify the brand, model, estimated price, look-alikes, and where to buy from a single photo. Take a picture or pick one from your library.

  • Legal (Sunglasses Identifier only): Privacy Policy · Terms of Use
  • Free tier: one identification per day. Premium includes unlimited identifications, ad-free use, and full on-device history (last 20 results).
  • Subscriptions: same as above — Apple manages billing and cancellation.

Identifications are AI-generated for casual reference only — not for authentication, appraisal, resale, or anti-counterfeit verification. Details are in the Sunglasses Identifier Terms of Use.

Presser Foot Identifier

Presser Foot Identifier is a separate app. Photograph a sewing-machine presser foot and get a report on what it is, how it attaches, what it is for, and the safety checks to make before you sew. It does not use the Sunglasses Identifier legal pages.

  • Legal: Privacy Policy · Terms of Use
  • Free tier: one successful identification every 24 hours, including the full report and up to three photos. No account is required.
  • Premium: unlimited identifications for $4.99 per week or $24.99 per year. There is no free trial on either plan.
  • Subscriptions: managed through Apple (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
