Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dmitriy Andrianov ("we," "us," "our"), based in the United States, handles information when you use Pesos to Dollars Conversion + or Euros to Dollars Conversion + (each an "App"). By using an App, you agree to this policy together with our Terms of Use.

We design the Apps to work with minimal personal data. We do not sell your personal information. What we process depends on which features you use.

Information we process

Local data on your device. Each App may store items such as conversion history, cached exchange rates, onboarding progress, free-conversion counters, and similar settings using your device storage. That information stays on the device unless you delete the App or clear data in the App.

Photos you submit for AI price reading. If you use camera or photo-library features to extract a price from an image, the App may send that image to our backend (for example a Supabase Edge Function) which forwards it to Google's Gemini (or similar) for processing. The image is used to generate a result and is not stored by us beyond what is necessary to return that result. Google's processing is governed by Google's terms and privacy notice. Only submit photos you are allowed to share.

Exchange rates. The Apps request reference rates from third-party APIs over the network. Those requests may include technical metadata usual for HTTP requests; they do not need your name or email.

Purchases. If you subscribe, Apple processes payment. We use providers such as RevenueCat to validate entitlements. They receive transaction-related identifiers needed to operate purchases.

Support email. If you email us, we receive your address, message content, and standard email metadata.
